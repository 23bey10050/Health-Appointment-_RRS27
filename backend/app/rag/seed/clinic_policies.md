# Clinic Policies

## Cancellation policy

Patients may cancel a confirmed appointment free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled
time, either through the patient portal, the voice assistant, or by calling the front desk.
Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the appointment are still accepted at no charge —
City Care Clinic does not charge no-show or late-cancellation fees — but we ask patients to
cancel as early as possible so the slot can be offered to someone else. Repeated late
cancellations may be flagged by the clinic for a courtesy call from the front desk, not a
penalty.

## Rescheduling policy

Appointments can be rescheduled any time before the original slot starts, subject to the new
doctor and time being available. Rescheduling through the patient portal or voice assistant is
instant and does not require speaking to staff. If a doctor becomes unavailable due to leave or
an emergency, the clinic proactively cancels the affected appointments and offers up to three
alternative times, by email, with a one-click rebooking link.

## Late arrival policy

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes after their scheduled time may need to wait for the next
available slot with their doctor, since later appointments will already be underway. Patients
running late should call the front desk so the doctor can be informed; some doctors are able to
accommodate a short delay, others are not, depending on the day's schedule.

## Walk-in policy

City Care Main Hospital and City Care South Hospital both accept walk-ins for general medicine on
a first-come, first-served basis, subject to same-day availability. City Care North Clinic is
appointment-only. Walk-ins are never accepted for specialist consultations — those always require
a booked appointment.

## Telemedicine

Video consultations are available for follow-up appointments and non-urgent concerns, at the
treating doctor's discretion. A doctor may convert an in-person follow-up to video if a physical
examination isn't needed. Video consultations are not available for a patient's first visit with
a new doctor, and are never offered for anything the emergency escalation flow has flagged.

## Payment and billing

Consultation fees are payable at the time of the visit by cash, card, or UPI. City Care Clinic
does not currently process insurance claims directly — patients pay the consultation fee and are
given an itemised receipt suitable for reimbursement claims with their own insurer. See
insurance_and_billing.md for details on what a receipt includes and how reimbursement typically
works.

## Prescription refills

Refills for ongoing medication can be requested through the patient portal without a full
appointment, for medications a City Care doctor has already prescribed within the last 6 months.
Refill requests are reviewed by the prescribing doctor and typically approved or queried within
one business day. New medications, or anything outside that 6-month window, require a fresh
appointment.

## Recording and privacy for the voice assistant

Voice conversations with the appointment assistant are recorded and transcribed to coordinate
care between the assistant, the clinic, and the treating doctor. Patients are asked for explicit
consent before a voice session starts, are told what is recorded and why, and can withdraw
consent and end the session at any time. Raw audio is deleted after the retention period
configured by the clinic; the text transcript is retained as part of the medical record, the same
as a nurse's intake notes would be.
