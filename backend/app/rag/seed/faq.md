# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I choose which doctor I see?

Yes. You can search by specialisation, by hospital, or by a specific doctor's name, and see each
doctor's next available slot before booking. If you don't have a preference, the voice assistant
can suggest a doctor based on your symptoms and the earliest availability.

## Can I see the same doctor for a follow-up?

Yes, and it's usually recommended — the booking flow lets you book directly with the doctor you
saw last time. If that doctor isn't available soon enough, you can choose someone else in the
same specialisation instead.

## What happens if my doctor goes on leave before my appointment?

The clinic cancels the affected appointment and emails you up to three alternative times — either
with the same doctor after they're back, or with another doctor in the same specialisation at the
same hospital, within a few days of your original time. The email includes a one-click link to
rebook instantly.

## Do you treat emergencies?

City Care Main Hospital and City Care South Hospital both have emergency departments. If you
describe symptoms that sound like a medical emergency while talking to the voice assistant, it
will stop the normal booking flow immediately, give you emergency guidance, and connect you to
urgent care rather than a routine appointment. For a life-threatening emergency, always call 108
(ambulance) or 112 (unified emergency number) directly rather than waiting for a booking.

## How do I get my prescription refilled?

Use the refill request feature in the patient portal for any medication a City Care doctor
prescribed within the last 6 months. The prescribing doctor reviews and approves refill requests,
usually within one business day.

## Will I get a summary after my visit?

Yes. After your doctor submits their notes, an AI-generated summary is prepared in plain language
covering what was discussed, your medication schedule, what to do and avoid, and warning signs to
watch for. Your doctor reviews and approves this summary before it's emailed to you — it's never
sent automatically without a clinician checking it first.

## Can the voice assistant tell me what's wrong with me?

No. The voice assistant gathers your symptoms and history, and routes you to the right doctor —
it does not diagnose, and it won't guess at a condition or recommend medication, even if asked
directly. Anything like that is intentionally left to the doctor at your visit.

## What if I need to speak to a person instead of the assistant?

You can switch to typing at any point during a voice session, and you can always ask to be
transferred to a human, which creates a callback request handled by the front desk.

## Is my conversation with the voice assistant private?

Your conversation is used only for care coordination — reaching the right doctor, preparing your
visit, and keeping a record your doctor can review. See clinic_policies.md for the full recording
and retention policy.
