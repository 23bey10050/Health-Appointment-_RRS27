# Insurance and Billing

## How billing works at City Care Clinic

City Care Clinic is a fee-for-service clinic: patients pay the consultation fee directly at the
time of the visit, rather than the clinic billing an insurer on the patient's behalf. This applies
across all three locations and all specialisations.

## What's on the receipt

Every visit generates an itemised receipt showing the consultation fee, the doctor's name and
registration number, the date and time of the visit, the diagnosis code where applicable, and
the clinic's GST registration details. This receipt is what most insurers require to process a
reimbursement claim, whether it's a corporate health policy, a personal health insurance plan, or
a government scheme that reimburses after the fact.

## Reimbursement

Because the clinic doesn't submit claims directly, reimbursement timelines and rules depend
entirely on the patient's own insurer — City Care Clinic has no visibility into whether a specific
plan covers a specific visit, and cannot check insurance eligibility in advance. Patients with
questions about whether their plan covers a particular consultation should contact their insurer
directly before the visit.

## Cashless treatment

City Care Clinic does not currently offer cashless treatment arrangements with any insurer. All
consultations are paid for directly, with a receipt provided for the patient to claim
reimbursement afterward.

## Consultation fees

Consultation fees vary by doctor and specialisation and are shown when booking, before the
appointment is confirmed. Fees are set per visit, not per diagnosis or per prescription — a
follow-up visit is billed at the same consultation fee as a first visit, unless the doctor's
listed fee differentiates between the two (shown at booking time).

## Corporate and group billing

Patients visiting under a corporate wellness arrangement should mention this when booking, as
some corporate arrangements have a preferred doctor list or a different fee schedule. The front
desk can confirm whether a specific corporate arrangement applies before the consultation fee is
charged.
